# Playtest 01 — Orientation and feedback

**Status: not run. No human observations recorded yet.**

M2.1–M2.4 are implemented. M2.5 requires 3–5 new players and fixes for the top observed problems before M3. Prefer five players so the design target, four of five finishing Orientation without help, can be checked. The owner's earlier movement/cue feedback remains unspecified and is not marked resolved.

## Start each player

1. In Godot 4.7.2, open `game/orientation/orientation.tscn` and press F6. This replays Orientation even when a previous player completed it. Completing or skipping it enters the solo shift.
2. Ask the player to play. Give no controls, explanation or solutions. Observe where they hesitate; let the idle hints appear naturally.
3. Record help requests and skips. If assistance is necessary, give it and record exactly what was said.
4. Let them attempt a shift. Observe pickup, delivery, Heat, results and Play Again. F10 opens Settings and pauses the offline shift; Esc closes Settings. Key prompts follow remapped controls.
5. Afterwards ask: What was your goal? How did you learn to pick up and deliver? What did the hound's sparks mean? Were movement/mouse response comfortable? Which cues were unclear or missed?

Keep player labels anonymous. No telemetry is enabled.
