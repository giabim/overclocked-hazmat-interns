# M1 review — solo prototype

M0 and M1.1–M1.3, M1.5–M1.6 are complete. M1.4 and M1.7 are implemented and await the human checks specified in the roadmap. Do not mark M1 approved automatically.

## Play

Open `project.godot` in Godot 4.7.2 and press F6 on `game/main.tscn` (or F5 for the project). On this Windows checkout, the engine is already installed; from `godot/`, PowerShell can also run:

```powershell
& ./.tools/Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe --path .
```

WASD moves; mouse looks; E picks up a nearby cyan canister or delivers it at the cyan east airlock. Esc releases/recaptures the cursor. Carrying is slower. Deliver three canisters to meet the solo quota; stay at the airlock to extract. Play Again resets the shift; Main Menu opens the solo start screen.
